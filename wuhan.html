Related Questions

Everything you need to know about treatment at our Wuhan clinic

How much does a hair transplant cost at the Wuhan clinic?
The final price depends on graft count and technique. You will receive a clear, all-inclusive quote at your free consultation. At our Wuhan clinic you receive a transparent, all-inclusive quote after a free consultation — no hidden fees. Contact us for a personalized estimate.
Where is the Wuhan clinic located?
Our Wuhan clinic is one of the clinics in our nationwide network, in a city known as River City of Central China. Contact us by WeChat or email and our coordinator will send the exact address and step-by-step directions.
Do the doctors at the Wuhan clinic speak English?
Yes. Our Wuhan team includes English-speaking coordinators and doctors who will guide you through consultation, surgery and aftercare in clear, simple English.
Can international patients be treated at the Wuhan clinic?
Absolutely. The Wuhan clinic regularly welcomes patients from around the world and can help with appointment scheduling, travel guidance and remote aftercare.
What techniques are used at the Wuhan clinic?
We use microneedle FUE and implanter pens with 0.6–1.0mm instruments for natural density and a quicker recovery.
How long does the procedure take at the Wuhan clinic?
Most sessions take 4–8 hours depending on graft count. Most patients return to their hotel the same day and resume light activity within a few days.
How soon will I see results after treatment in Wuhan?
New growth usually starts at 3–4 months, with the final, mature result around 12–18 months. We schedule follow-ups to track your progress.
Is the procedure painful?
Local anaesthesia keeps you comfortable throughout. Most patients feel only mild soreness for a few days, easily controlled with the medication we provide.
Are the results permanent?
Yes. Grafts are taken from the genetically resistant donor zone, where hair is programmed to keep growing.
Can I wash my hair after the procedure?
Thanks to the fine microneedle incisions, most patients can gently wash their hair after 24 hours. Our Wuhan team will give you full aftercare instructions.
Will I need to stay in Wuhan after surgery?
Most international patients stay 2–3 days in Wuhan for the procedure and an initial check-up. Remote follow-ups are arranged after you return home.
How do I book a consultation at the Wuhan clinic?
Simply contact us via WeChat, WhatsApp or email. Our Wuhan coordinator will arrange a free consultation, either in person or by video call.
Can the Wuhan clinic help with travel and accommodation?
Yes. We can provide invitation letters, airport guidance and hotel recommendations to make your trip to Wuhan smooth and stress-free.
What payment methods are accepted in Wuhan?
We accept major cards, bank transfers and cash, and provide a clear written invoice. Your Wuhan quote covers surgery, medication, aftercare and follow-up.
Why choose the Wuhan clinic over other providers?
Barley brings together two decades of experience, 10+ patented technologies and a network of 40+ clinics, with clear pricing and attentive aftercare.
How do I get to the Wuhan clinic from the airport?
Most international patients fly into Wuhan Tianhe International Airport (WUH). From there the airport expressway reaches the city centre in about 40 minutes. Our Wuhan coordinator can share a precise door-to-door route and arrange an airport pickup on request.
What is there to do in Wuhan around my appointment?
Wuhan is the river city of central china, with plenty to see between visits. Patients often enjoy the Yellow Crane Tower, East Lake and the Yangtze River Bridge. We can suggest a light itinerary that fits comfortably around your recovery.
