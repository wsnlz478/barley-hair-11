Explore Chengdu While You're Here

Your hair restoration journey can be so much more. While you're visiting our Chengdu clinic, take time to discover the city's most unforgettable sights — high-quality care and an unforgettable trip, all in one.

Wuhou Shrine

Related Questions

Everything you need to know about treatment at our Chengdu clinic

How much does a hair transplant cost at the Chengdu clinic?
Costs are calculated per graft and by technique, with a transparent quote provided after your consultation. At our Chengdu clinic you receive a transparent, all-inclusive quote after a free consultation — no hidden fees. Contact us for a personalized estimate.
Where is the Chengdu clinic located?
Our Chengdu clinic is one of the clinics in our nationwide network, in a city known as Panda & Food Capital. Send us a WeChat or email and we will confirm the address and how to reach us in Chengdu.
Do the doctors at the Chengdu clinic speak English?
Yes. Our Chengdu team includes English-speaking coordinators and doctors who will guide you through consultation, surgery and aftercare in clear, simple English.
Can international patients be treated at the Chengdu clinic?
Absolutely. The Chengdu clinic regularly welcomes patients from around the world and can help with appointment scheduling, travel guidance and remote aftercare.
What techniques are used at the Chengdu clinic?
We place grafts with 0.6–1.0mm implanter pens, controlling direction and depth for natural-looking density.
How long does the procedure take at the Chengdu clinic?
Most sessions take 4–8 hours depending on graft count. The procedure is outpatient, so you can return to your hotel the same day and rest for a few days.
How soon will I see results after treatment in Chengdu?
Visible growth typically starts after 3–4 months, and results mature over the following year. We schedule follow-ups to track your progress.
Is the procedure painful?
Local anaesthesia keeps you comfortable throughout. Most patients describe only mild discomfort afterwards, which settles quickly with medication.
Are the results permanent?
Yes. The donor area supplies follicles that are naturally resistant to pattern hair loss, so results are long-lasting.
Can I wash my hair after the procedure?
The tiny incisions allow gentle shampooing from around 24 hours after treatment. Our Chengdu team will give you full aftercare instructions.
Will I need to stay in Chengdu after surgery?
Most international patients stay 2–3 days in Chengdu for the procedure and an initial check-up. Remote follow-ups are arranged after you return home.
How do I book a consultation at the Chengdu clinic?
Simply contact us via WeChat, WhatsApp or email. Our Chengdu coordinator will arrange a free consultation, either in person or by video call.
Can the Chengdu clinic help with travel and accommodation?
Yes. We can provide invitation letters, airport guidance and hotel recommendations to make your trip to Chengdu smooth and stress-free.
What payment methods are accepted in Chengdu?
We take major cards, bank transfers and cash, and give you a written breakdown of costs. Your Chengdu quote covers surgery, medication, aftercare and follow-up.
Why choose the Chengdu clinic over other providers?
Two decades of clinical work, 10+ patented technologies and 40+ clinics are the foundation of our transparent care.
How do I get to the Chengdu clinic from the airport?
Most international patients fly into Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU). From there the airport express and metro link to downtown in about an hour. Our Chengdu coordinator can share a precise door-to-door route and arrange an airport pickup on request.
What is there to do in Chengdu around my appointment?
Chengdu is the panda & Food Capital, with plenty to see between visits. Patients often enjoy the Giant Panda Breeding Centre, Jinli Ancient Street and a Sichuan hotpot dinner. We can put together a relaxed schedule around your treatment and check-ups.
Is Chengdu a good city to recover in?
Yes. Chengdu is the capital of Sichuan province and is beloved for its giant pandas, fiery hotpot and famously relaxed pace of life. Teahouses, ancient streets and a strong local food culture make it one of China's most enjoyable cities for visitors. It is also a major western hub with two international airports and growing international hotel options. Most patients stay 2–3 days for the procedure and first check-up, and find Chengdu a comfortable and interesting place to rest.
Can you help with a medical visa and travel plans for Chengdu?
Absolutely. Our Chengdu team provides invitation letters and travel guidance for patients flying in, including advice on reaching us from Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU). We will fit your consultations and surgery into a practical schedule.
