Related Questions

Everything you need to know about treatment at our Harbin clinic

How much does a hair transplant cost at the Harbin clinic?
Fees vary with graft count and technique, and we provide a full written quote after assessment. At our Harbin clinic you receive a transparent, all-inclusive quote after a free consultation — no hidden fees. Contact us for a personalized estimate.
Where is the Harbin clinic located?
Our Harbin clinic is one of the clinics in our nationwide network, in a city known as Ice City of the North. Message us on WeChat or email and we will send precise directions to the Harbin clinic.
Do the doctors at the Harbin clinic speak English?
Yes. Our Harbin team includes English-speaking coordinators and doctors who will guide you through consultation, surgery and aftercare in clear, simple English.
Can international patients be treated at the Harbin clinic?
Absolutely. The Harbin clinic regularly welcomes patients from around the world and can help with appointment scheduling, travel guidance and remote aftercare.
What techniques are used at the Harbin clinic?
Microneedle FUE with 0.6–1.0mm implanter pens allows controlled angle and depth, for natural results and quicker recovery.
How long does the procedure take at the Harbin clinic?
Most sessions take 4–8 hours depending on graft count. Same-day discharge is standard; light activity is typically fine again within a few days.
How soon will I see results after treatment in Harbin?
Expect first signs of growth at 3–4 months and a stable, mature outcome by 12–18 months. We schedule follow-ups to track your progress.
Is the procedure painful?
Local anaesthesia keeps you comfortable throughout. Discomfort is generally limited to mild tenderness for a few days and is straightforward to manage.
Are the results permanent?
Yes. Donor follicles come from the stable zone at the back of the scalp and keep their natural growth pattern.
Can I wash my hair after the procedure?
Gentle washing is normally possible within 24 hours with this minimally invasive technique. Our Harbin team will give you full aftercare instructions.
Will I need to stay in Harbin after surgery?
Most international patients stay 2–3 days in Harbin for the procedure and an initial check-up. Remote follow-ups are arranged after you return home.
How do I book a consultation at the Harbin clinic?
Simply contact us via WeChat, WhatsApp or email. Our Harbin coordinator will arrange a free consultation, either in person or by video call.
Can the Harbin clinic help with travel and accommodation?
Yes. We can provide invitation letters, airport guidance and hotel recommendations to make your trip to Harbin smooth and stress-free.
What payment methods are accepted in Harbin?
All major payment methods are accepted, and you will receive a detailed invoice. Your Harbin quote covers surgery, medication, aftercare and follow-up.
Why choose the Harbin clinic over other providers?
Our 20+ year track record, 10+ patented technologies and 40+ directly managed clinics underpin a transparent approach.
How do I get to the Harbin clinic from the airport?
Most international patients fly into Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB). From there the airport is roughly 45 minutes from the city centre. Our Harbin coordinator can share a precise door-to-door route and arrange an airport pickup on request.
What is there to do in Harbin around my appointment?
Harbin is the ice city of the north, with plenty to see between visits. Patients often enjoy Saint Sophia Cathedral, Central Street and the Ice and Snow World in winter. We are happy to recommend low-key activities that work alongside your recovery.
Is Harbin a good city to recover in?
Yes. Harbin is a northeastern city famous for its spectacular Ice and Snow World festival and a striking Russian Orthodox cathedral, reminders of its historic Russian influence. Central Street is lined with European-style buildings and cafés. Winters are cold and festive, while summers are pleasantly cool, offering a distinctive seasonal appeal. Most patients stay 2–3 days for the procedure and first check-up, and find Harbin a comfortable and interesting place to rest.
Can you help with a medical visa and travel plans for Harbin?
Absolutely. Our Harbin team provides invitation letters and travel guidance for patients flying in, including advice on reaching us from Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB). Our team will schedule appointments around your travel plans.
